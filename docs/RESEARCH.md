## Frameworks and authentication

- [Astro on Workers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/framework-guides/web-apps/astro/) supports on-demand rendering and islands and is a strong content-site choice. A separate app framework is not necessary for our server-driven CRUD workflow.
- [Next.js on Workers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/framework-guides/web-apps/nextjs/) currently recommends **vinext**, explicitly marked beta in the official source, and documents other deployment paths. We do not choose a beta adapter or an oversized React runtime for this MVP.
- [Hono’s official Workers guide](https://hono.dev/docs/getting-started/cloudflare-workers) was read through [its official repository](https://github.com/honojs/website/blob/main/docs/getting-started/cloudflare-workers.md). It maps directly to Workers bindings and standards-based APIs, with server JSX rendering.
- React alone needs a routing/rendering/deployment strategy. It is an option for later complex admin widgets, not required for a responsive CMS with server-rendered forms.
- [Cloudflare Access JWT validation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/access-controls/applications/http-apps/authorization-cookie/validating-json/) documents signature validation, team issuer and audience checks. Use an invited identity provider and optional IdP MFA, not passwords in a 10 ms Worker. Access is a separate product: its current free seat allowance was **not verified** from the accessible source. Confirm the chosen account plan and small admin team fit before enabling it.

## Pakistan payments: research boundary

Owner preference: Pakistan operations, PKR and USD, Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank transfers and Payoneer. Official destinations attempted: [Easypaisa gateway](https://easypaisa.com.pk/online-payment-gateway/), [JazzCash gateway](https://www.jazzcash.com.pk/corporate/online-payment-gateway/), [Payoneer client payments](https://www.payoneer.com/get-paid-by-clients/), [Stripe country support](https://stripe.com/global). All were blocked, so current merchant eligibility, fees, onboarding and webhook contracts are unresolved.

No provider is enabled. Wallet merchant gateways require approved business accounts and integrations; ordinary wallet transfers cannot be treated as an automatic gateway. Payoneer invoicing/payment requests must be checked against the account’s permitted business use, payer types and country restrictions. Direct Stripe eligibility must be checked; do not assume a Pakistani business can open an account. Safepay/PayFast are candidates for follow-up official research, not validated recommendations. Do not create an overseas entity or invent merchant IDs as a workaround.

Launch can begin with **inquiry → agreed quote → invoice → manually verified bank/wallet receipt → order confirmation**, if legally and operationally approved. An uploaded screenshot or payment reference alone does not prove payment. Automatic checkout remains disabled until a real provider adapter passes signature, replay and amount/currency tests. Provider fees, foreign exchange, withdrawals, transaction taxes and verification requirements must be disclosed after official confirmation.
